Drop your portraits here, named 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... N.jpg
then set  photos.count = N  in ../people.js

Tips
- Head-and-shoulders crops, roughly square. 600 px or larger looks best:
  the same file is used on the wall AND as the big collage portrait.
- The wall mirrors / re-crops them so repeats are hard to spot, but the more
  distinct photos you add, the better the mosaic reads.
